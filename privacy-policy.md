# Privacy Policy for DayYes

**Last updated: September 22, 2026**

DayYes ("the App") is a personal daily reflection application developed and published by DayYes.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

DayYes does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the App.

The App does not send your daily entries or notes to DayYes servers. DayYes does not operate a backend service for storing your journal data.

## 2. Data Stored on Your Device

Information you enter into DayYes, including your daily status and optional notes, is stored locally on your device.

This information remains on your device unless you choose to export or otherwise share it using functionality provided by your device.

Deleting the App may remove locally stored DayYes data from your device.

## 3. Data Export

DayYes may allow you to export your data.

Exporting data is initiated by you. Once exported, the resulting file is handled by the location, application, or service you choose to save or share it with. DayYes does not automatically receive a copy of your exported data.

## 4. Notifications

DayYes may provide optional local reminders to help you remember to record your day.

If you enable reminders, the App may request permission to send notifications and may use Android system functionality to schedule them.

Reminder processing occurs on your device. DayYes does not use notification functionality to collect or transmit your journal entries or notes.

You can disable reminders in the App or manage notification permissions through your device settings.

## 5. Analytics and Advertising

DayYes does not include third-party advertising.

DayYes does not use third-party analytics services to track your activity in the App.

## 6. Data Sharing

DayYes does not sell your personal information.

DayYes does not transmit your daily entries or notes to the developer or share them with third parties.

If you explicitly choose to export or share information, it will be provided to the destination selected by you.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your DayYes entries are stored locally on your device.

You can edit or delete individual entries using the App. You may also remove locally stored application data by using your device's application settings or by uninstalling the App, subject to your device's operating system and backup behavior.

Because DayYes does not maintain user accounts or store your journal entries on DayYes servers, there is no remote account or journal-data deletion process.

## 8. Children's Privacy

DayYes is a general-purpose personal reflection application and is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

DayYes does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a DayYes-operated server.

## 9. Security

DayYes is designed to keep your journal information locally on your device. The security of locally stored information also depends on the security of your device and operating system.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when DayYes features or data practices change.

Any changes will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
